# CBMAP_methylation_QTL
This repository contains the analysis code for ***East Asian brain methylation QTL atlas reveals ancestry- and tissue-specific genetic regulation of neuropsychiatric traits***, one of the epigenomic investigations from the China Brain Multi-omics Atlas Project (CBMAP).
* We performed genome-wide cis- and trans-mQTL mapping in 1,018 EAS PFC samples profiled using the EPICv2 array and compared the resulting mQTL landscape with European-ancestry PFC and EAS peripheral blood mQTL datasets.
* We investigated the regulatory mechanisms underlying genetic control of DNA methylation by integrating mQTL signals with eQTL, caQTL, Hi-C, and other regulatory annotations.
* We performed cell-type interaction mQTL mapping to identify genetic effects on DNA methylation that vary across major brain cell types.
* We integrated mQTLs with GWAS summary statistics through colocalization analyses to identify ancestry- and tissue-specific genetic regulatory mechanisms underlying complex and neuropsychiatric traits.
* We integrated genetic information across EAS and European-ancestry populations using MA-FOCUS to improve the prioritization of putative causal CpGs and identify robust disease-associated methylation signals.
